CASE STUDY 1 / CATALYTIC DYNAMICS

WT-SpCas9 Structural Allostery & Checkpoint Kinetics

Doudna et al., Science | Cryo-EM & smFRET
🔬
The Thermodynamic Specificity Dilemma: WT-SpCas9 possesses an excessive binding affinity (ΔG° ≈ −22 to −25 kcal/mol) evolved to prevent viral escape mutations. This energetic buffer permits stable binding and off-target cleavage at non-cognate sites with 4–5 mismatches.
🔄
01. CONFORMATIONAL CHECKPOINT

Directional R-Loop & HNH 32 Å Rotation

Nucleation Trajectory: Heteroduplex formation propagates from Seed (pos 1–8) to Mid (9–14) and Distal (15–20).
Allosteric Trigger: At 14–16 bp, REC3 senses non-target strand displacement and triggers a concerted 32 Å HNH rotation.
Catalytic Alignment: Docks His840 within 3.5 Å of target scissile phosphate and activates RuvC catalytic triad (D10, E762, D986).
📊
02. KINETIC REJECTION

Mismatch Sensitivity Profile

Seed Mismatches (1–8): Abort R-loop nucleation; cleavage velocity drops to 1.1% (kobs = 0.002 s−¹).
Distal Mismatches (15–20): Retain up to 76.8% of maximal cleavage velocity in WT-SpCas9 due to energy buffer.

CASE STUDY 1 / FIDELITY PLATFORMS

Engineered High-Fidelity Nucleases: eSpCas9, HF1 & SuperFi

Joung et al., Nature | Slaymaker et al., Science
⚙️

🎯
STRATEGY A: NON-TARGET GROOVE

eSpCas9(1.1) (K848A / K1003A / R1060A)

Weakens binding to the displaced non-target strand. Mismatched heteroduplexes fail to overcome the strand separation barrier and rapidly re-anneal.

42.5x Specificity Ratio Gain
🛡️
STRATEGY B: TARGET CONTACT ATTENUATION

SpCas9-HF1 & SuperFi-Cas9

HF1 (N497A/R661A/Q695A/Q926A) removes 4 phosphate H-bonds. SuperFi-Cas9 stabilizes the REC3 loop (797-804), restoring on-target rate (kobs = 0.165 s−¹).

>1,000x SuperFi-Cas9 Specificity

CASE STUDY 3 / BASE EDITING

Precision Single-Base Transitions: CBE & ABE Architecture

Liu et al., Nature | Gaudelli et al., Nature
🧬
Chemical Pencil vs Scissors: Base editors fuse a catalytic deaminase to Cas9 nickase (nCas9-D10A). Instead of cutting double strands, they chemically convert single bases in a defined 4–5 nt window with <0.1% indels and zero translocations.
🧪
CYTIDINE BASE EDITORS (CBE)

rAPOBEC1 / evoAPOBEC + UGI (C•G → T•A)

Hydrolytic Deamination: Deaminates cytidine to uridine within protospacer positions 4–8.
UGI Inhibition: Uracil DNA Glycosylase Inhibitor prevents base excision repair (BER).
Directed Nicking: nCas9 nicking directs cellular mismatch repair to copy U template.
⚡
ADENINE BASE EDITORS (ABE)

Evolved TadA-8e Deaminase (A•T → G•C)

Laboratory Evolution: Evolved from bacterial tRNA adenosine deaminase to accept single-stranded DNA.
Ultra-Fast Kinetics: TadA-8e increases catalytic deamination rate by 590-fold.
Therapeutic Target: Capable of reversing ~47% of pathogenic human point mutations in ClinVar.

CASE STUDY 3 / PRIME EDITING

Prime Editing: Search-and-Replace Word Processing (PEmax & twinPE)

Anzalone, Liu et al., Nature | Chen et al., Cell
✍️
The Universal Genomic Word Processor: Prime Editing writes new genetic information directly into a target site using an engineered Reverse Transcriptase and a prime editing guide RNA (pegRNA) without requiring double-strand breaks or donor DNA templates.
🧩
pegRNA DUAL-FUNCTION STRUCTURE

PBS + Reverse Transcriptase Template (RTT)

Primer Binding Site (PBS): 13 nt region that hybridizes to the nicked 3' non-target genomic flap.
RT Template (RTT): Encodes the programmed substitution, insertion (up to 40 bp), or deletion.
Flap Equilibration: 3' edited flap displaces 5' unedited flap; cellular FEN1 resolves flap junction.
🚀
PE ARCHITECTURE EVOLUTION

PE2 → PEmax → twinPE Recombination

PEmax (Cell 2021): Codon-optimized RT, MLH1 dominant-negative inhibition; yields 55–85% efficiency.
twinPE (Nat. Biotechnol. 2021): Dual pegRNAs write dual flaps; integrates kilobase payloads with recombinases.

CASE STUDY 5 / PAM RESTRICTION

Dismantling PAM Restriction: SpG, SpRY & Near-PAMless Nucleases

🔓
Unlocking the Entire Genome: Canonical WT-SpCas9 requires a 5'-NGG PAM (accessible at ~1/16 genomic sites). Directed evolution of the PAM-Interacting Domain (PID) generated SpG (5'-NGN) and near-PAMless SpRY (5'-NRN / NYN), unlocking >99% of genomic target space.
🧬
PID DIRECTED EVOLUTION

SpG (NGN) & SpRY (NRN/NYN) Engineering

SpG Variant: 5 mutations (D1135L/S1136W/G1218K/E1219Q/R1335Q); recognizes all 5'-NGN sites (~50% genome).
SpRY Variant: 11 mutations; recognizes 5'-NRN > NYN; targets >99% of nuclear loci.
⚖️
OFF-TARGET MITIGATION: SpRY-HF1

Balancing Relaxed PAM with High Fidelity

Unconstrained PAM recognition increases potential off-target search space. Coupling SpRY with high-fidelity mutations (HF1 or eSpCas9) restores single-locus discrimination.

>99% Nuclear Genome Targetable Access Scope

CASE STUDY 5 / MITOCHONDRIAL EDITING

Organellar Genome Editing: RNA-Free DdCBE Base Editing

⚡
Overcoming the Mitochondrial RNA Barrier: The inner mitochondrial membrane is impermeable to guide RNAs. DdCBE circumvents this barrier using a protein-only TALE targeting system fused to a split bacterial interbacterial toxin deaminase (split-DddA_tox) to execute C•G → T•A transitions in double-stranded mtDNA.
🔬
PROTEIN-ONLY ARCHITECTURE

MTS-TALE-Split-DddA_tox + UGI

Mitochondrial Targeting (MTS): Cleavable N-terminal leader peptide directs import via TOM/TIM translocases.
Split Deaminase Safety: DddA is split into inactive halves (N-term and C-term) to prevent cellular cytotoxicity.
dsDNA Deamination: Active holoprotein reconstitutes only when both TALE arrays bind adjacent mtDNA sites.
🔋
THERAPEUTIC CORRECTION

MELAS, MERRF & LHON Cybrid Rescue

Corrects m.8344A>G (MERRF tRNA-Lys) and m.3243A>G (MELAS tRNA-Leu). Rescues mitochondrial respiration without inducing double-strand breaks or mtDNA depletion.
