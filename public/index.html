02 / MECHANISMS & REPAIR

Natural vs Engineered CRISPR & The Two Repair Crews

Bacterial Defense to Precision Editing
🛠️
The Core Concept: CRISPR only makes the cut. The cell's repair teams choose between a quick patch (NHEJ knockout) or a careful template rewrite (HDR).
🧬
SYSTEM EVOLUTION

Natural Defense vs. Engineered Single-Guide

Natural Bacterial Pathway: Requires 2 separate RNAs (crRNA + tracrRNA) cleaved by RNase III.
Engineered Lab System: Fused into a single chimeric sgRNA, creating an all-in-one plasmid.
2 RNAs → 1 sgRNA Platform Engineering Milestone
🩹
CREW 01: EMERGENCY GLUE

NHEJ (Non-Homologous End Joining)

Mechanism: Cell quickly stitches broken ends without template, creating +1/-2bp frameshifts.
Outcome: Disables harmful disease-causing genes permanently with >90% knockout efficiency.
>90% Efficiency Gene Knockout Mode
📐
CREW 02: MASTER BLUEPRINT

HDR (Homology-Directed Repair)

Mechanism: Supplied with synthetic donor DNA template matching flanking sequence arms.
Outcome: Cell enzymes flawlessly copy donor sequence to correct mutations with 100% fidelity.
100% Accuracy Precision Gene Correction
NATURAL BACTERIAL PATHWAY VS ENGINEERED SYSTEM
⚡ Cellular Repair Switcher
🩹 NHEJ Mode Active: Creates frameshift indels → turns OFF disease genes.
